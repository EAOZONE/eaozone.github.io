Research · Persona AI · Aug 2026–present

Perceptive Locomotion

I investigated rewards and terrain observations for humanoid walking on stairs, slopes, and uneven ground.

Reward design & foot placement

  • I adapted foot-placement rewards in an existing humanoid walking environment, comparing penalties based on the largest terrain measurement with ones that combined measurements across the foot.
  • Course completion improved from 80% to 95% on unfamiliar terrain over 1,000 simulation episodes per policy, with somewhat slower walking.
  • The policy I trained was tested on hardware, where it successfully guided a humanoid up and down a full flight of stairs.

Perception & policy training

  • I compared two complete approaches: a height-map MLP with actor–critic training and a noise curriculum, and a depth-image CNN trained from a teacher with an ideal height map.
  • In a separate evaluation of 100 simulation episodes per policy, success was 90% for the height-map approach and 95% for depth. Success required reaching the destination at an average speed of at least 0.54 m/s. Neither policy fell; unsuccessful trials were too slow.
  • I traced the loss of grated-stair detail to depth-image resizing by comparing recorded sensor footage with policy inputs.
  • I evaluated an additional LiDAR/depth policy in simulation. It improved grated-stair ascent and descent but performed worse on ordinary stairs.
